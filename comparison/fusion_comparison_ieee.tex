\begin{abstract}
We compare 12 verified, high-impact-factor IEEE methods for hyperspectral--multispectral
(HSI-MSI) image fusion, emphasizing model-based, unsupervised, and blind formulations
that are most related to physics-informed fusion. For each method we report the venue,
type, fusion scale, and per-dataset SOTA (PSNR / SAM) under the Wald protocol, together
with a consolidated per-dataset benchmark. Values marked $\approx$ are OCR recoveries
from rasterized result tables and are approximate; entries marked ``see paper'' were not
machine-extractable. Full tables (including real / no-GT datasets) are provided in the
companion document \texttt{COMPARISON.md}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
HSI-MSI fusion reconstructs a high-spatial-resolution hyperspectral image from a
low-spatial-resolution HSI and a high-spatial-resolution MSI. Recent IEEE work spans
supervised deep networks, unsupervised/unrolled model-based methods, and blind
zero-shot formulations. This paper benchmarks 12 high-impact IEEE publications
(2024--2026) most related to model-based and blind fusion.

\section{Discussion}
Among the compared methods, the model-based / unrolled approaches (SCALMU, BHSR-Net,
Detail-Injection, SHOTUN, Equivariant) and the blind causal method report competitive
PSNR/SAM without (or with minimal) supervised training, which is most relevant to
physics-informed fusion with identifiability and uncertainty control. BFMM attains the
highest CAVE $\times$4 PSNR (53.41 dB) among the listed methods. Exact SSIM/ERGAS
figures for the recent methods are frequently omitted in the source PDFs and are left
for future acquisition.
