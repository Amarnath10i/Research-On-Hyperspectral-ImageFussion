\caption{ExactFuse. (a)~The HQS iteration of Section~\ref{sec:hqs} unrolled for three stages. Once per image, the operator parameters and noise levels are given or estimated from the observation pair (Theorem~\ref{thm:ident}) and the exact operators and their eigenbasis are built (Theorem~\ref{thm:kron}); every stage passes its decoder features to the next (memory). (b)~Inside a stage, the data step (Fig.~\ref{fig:dstep}) makes the estimate agree with both observations, and the prior adds a residual~$\Delta_k$. The prior is a three-level U-Net (transformer blocks and channels per level; strided convolutions halve the resolution, transposed convolutions double it); all its inputs lie in the hyperspectral bands, and its last convolution starts at zero. (c)~Every block is a Restormer block~\cite{zamir2022restormer}: layer normalization (LN), transposed attention between the $C'$ channels of each head, whose cost is linear in the number of pixels, and a gated feed-forward network, each in a residual branch. The supplementary material lists every layer.}
